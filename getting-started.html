Getting Started

Everything you need to start managing bookings in At9 — from setting up your space to taking your first booking.

1

Setting up your entities

Entities are the bookable resources in your business — rooms, tables, kennels, treatment rooms, or any space or asset your customers can reserve. You need at least one entity before you can take bookings.

To add or manage your entities, open the app and go to Admin → Entities. From there you can:

  • Add a new entity — give it a name, type, and maximum occupancy.
  • Edit an existing entity to update its name or capacity.
  • Reorder entities by dragging them into the position you want — this controls the order they appear in your schedule.
💡

Tip: Use specific names for your entities — "Garden Room" rather than "Room 1" — so they're easy to identify at a glance on your schedule.

2

Navigating the schedule

The schedule is your central view for everything happening in your business. Each column represents one of your entities, and each row is a time slot. Bookings appear as coloured blocks that span their start and end times.

  • Scroll left or right to move between days.
  • Tap the date header to jump to a specific month or date.
  • Tap a booking block to open its details in a panel.
  • Tap an empty slot to start a new booking for that time and entity.
💡

Tip: Use the date picker at the top of the schedule to jump straight to a future date — useful when checking availability weeks ahead.

3

Adding a booking

You can start a new booking in two ways:

  • From the schedule: tap the + button, or tap directly on an empty slot for a given entity and time.
  • From anywhere: tap the floating add button (bottom right of the main view).

In the booking form, fill in:

  • Customer — search for an existing customer or add a new one.
  • Entity — the room, table, or resource being reserved.
  • Start and end date/time — At9 automatically checks availability for the chosen entity as you fill in the dates.

If the slot is already taken, you'll see a conflict warning before you can save. Once saved, the booking appears on your schedule immediately.

💡

Checking availability: switch to the Availability tab within the booking form to see which slots are free across all your entities for the chosen dates — handy when a customer isn't fixed on a specific room.

4

Updating a booking

To edit a booking, tap it on the schedule to open the booking panel, then tap Edit. The same booking form opens with all current details pre-filled.

You can update:

  • Start and end date or time.
  • The assigned entity (room, table, etc.).
  • Customer details.
  • Any notes attached to the booking.

At9 checks availability again when you save, so you'll be warned if your changes create a conflict with another booking.

5

Moving a booking

You can move a booking to a different time or entity directly on the schedule without opening the edit form.

  • Press and hold a booking block until it lifts.
  • Drag it to the new time slot or entity column.
  • Release to drop it in place — At9 saves the change automatically.
💡

Tip: If a customer calls to move to a different room, drag the booking across to the new entity column in seconds — no need to open the edit form at all.

6

Cancelling a booking

To cancel a booking, tap it on the schedule to open the booking panel, then tap Cancel. You'll be asked to confirm before anything is deleted.

Once confirmed, the booking is permanently removed and the slot becomes available for new bookings immediately.

💡

Note: Cancellation is permanent. If you think you might need a booking again, consider updating its dates instead of cancelling it.
