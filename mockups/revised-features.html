FAQs

Questions, answered.

What is At9? +
At9 is a booking management app for businesses with rooms or tables to fill. Create and manage bookings, track customers, and see your availability at a glance — all from your phone or tablet.
Which businesses is At9 built for? +
At9 is built for any business that takes room or table bookings — restaurants, pubs, hotels, catteries, kennels, and any other service with a bookable space. If you're still managing a paper diary or a spreadsheet, At9 can replace it.
Does At9 work on phones and tablets? +
Yes — At9 is available on iOS and Android, on both phones and tablets. The same login works everywhere and your data syncs in real time.
Can my whole team use it? +
Yes. The Team plan supports multiple staff with role-based permissions, so front-of-house, managers and owners each see what they need.
Is there a free trial? +
Yes — you can try At9 free with no credit card required. Upgrade only when it's working for you.
What happens to my data if I cancel? +
Your data is yours. You can export it any time, and we'll never sell it. If you cancel, you keep access to a read-only export for 30 days.
