Get the app

You're connected

Your Stripe account is now linked to At9. You can head back to the app — there's nothing else to do on this page.

  1. 1 Open Settings → Payments in the At9 app to check your status. It will show Active once Stripe has finished its checks.
  2. 2 Set what to charge on each room, table, service, class or event — open it and choose the Payment tab to take a deposit or the full amount.

Stripe sometimes needs a little longer to verify your details, and may ask for more information before you can take payments. If the app still shows Action needed, open Payments and it will tell you exactly what Stripe is waiting for.
