This is the website folder for At9 app (https://www.at9.app)

Content Context

At9.app is a mobile app, designed to run on Android and IOS
devices.

The app is used by organisations for managing bookings for rooms
and tables.

## Subscription tiers

⚠️ **Root [`CLAUDE.md`](../CLAUDE.md) is the source of truth for tiers.** This
section restates it for copywriting; if the two ever disagree, the root file
wins and this one is wrong.

**Taking bookings is free.** There is **no trial, no expiry and no card** — a
business can run its bookings on At9 indefinitely without paying. Never write
copy that implies a trial, a countdown, or that bookings stop when something
lapses.

There are two paid tiers:

- **Free** — everything needed to take and manage bookings: every room, table,
  class, event and appointment on one schedule; customers and their bookings;
  instant availability checking; email confirmations and updates; your own web
  and social booking link (`at9.app/book/SHORT_NAME`); messaging customers;
  customers accessing their own bookings; booking notes; deposits and payments
  by connecting Stripe.
- **Pro — £9.99/month** — booking questions, email reminders, and files against
  a customer or booking.
- **Team — £34.99/month** — everything in Pro, plus inviting your team to share
  the calendar and manage bookings, and holidays and sickness.

**Team is a superset of Pro**, so copy should say so rather than restating Pro's
features under Team.

**Stripe's transaction fees apply to each payment.** Say so wherever payments
are mentioned — a fee nobody disclosed is the kind of surprise that loses trust.

### Retired: Solo, and the 90-day trial

**Solo (£9.99/month) and the 90-day free trial no longer exist.** Both were
withdrawn: a plan that expires works against the goal, which is reach — get
businesses running their bookings here, earn from the ones that connect Stripe,
and sell Team to the ones that grow.

Existing Solo subscribers still exist and their SKU is still listed so a restore
recognises it, but **nothing new should ever be sold as Solo** and no page should
mention a trial.

⚠️ **£29.99 is not the current Team price.** It is what existing Team
subscribers still pay until the stores are changed; the advertised price is
**£34.99**. Prices live in App Store Connect and Google Play — changing a number
in this repo changes nobody's bill.

What type of companies use At9?
At9 is used by restaurants, bars, hotels, catteries and kennels - any
type of organisation that has rooms and tables looking to simplify
management of these.

What are the features of At9?

- Schedule for viewing bookings on a specific day, week or year
- Add and edit bookings in an instant
- Share web booking link on your social sites (Instagram, Facebook etc) as https://www.at9.app/book/my-org and accept bookings from the web.
- Instantly check availability
- Insant availability checking - No messing around searching for available rooms and tables.
- Set business operating hours
- Manage bookable hours for rooms and tables
- Add blocked recurring times when bookings aren't accessible
- Add closed periods
- Allow your customers to search and book a room or table
- Manage customers and their contact details
- Message customers about their bookings
- Delete and disable users
- Team subscription supports adding organisation users

Is there a trial period?

There is no trial, because there is nothing to trial *into* — taking bookings is
free, permanently, with no card. Pay only if you want booking questions,
reminders and files (Pro, £9.99/month) or to share the calendar with your team
(Team, £34.99/month).
